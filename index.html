Features

A mod, a hype-man and an editor in one

Everything runs automatically from the moment Habok goes live until the stream ends.

💬

AI chat buddy

Tag the bot and it answers in Hinglish/Urdu gamer style — game help, channel info, banter. Knows the regulars.

🛡️

Auto moderation

Scam links, abuse, caps and emoji floods are removed instantly. Strike 1 delete, strike 2 warning, strike 3 short timeout.

🔥

Modes

Mods switch between friendly, roast and serious. Roasts are for friends' gaming skills only — never Habok, never family.

👋

Welcomes & vibes

Greets first-time chatters, welcomes back regulars, and drops jokes, game facts and questions to keep chat alive.

🎬

Shorts finder

After each stream it spots chat reaction spikes, checks the recording, and suggests Shorts with timestamps and titles.

🚨

Safety net

Every reply is checked before it's posted, every stream is reviewed after, and a 31-test exam runs every week.

How it works

From chat message to reply in seconds

Simple, fast rules handle most messages. AI is only used when someone actually talks to the bot.

01ChatReads Habok's live chat through the official YouTube API.
02FilterSpam and abuse removed instantly — no AI needed.
03Commands!specs, !socials and friends answered straight away.
04AI agentTagged messages go to Gemini with the channel's facts and rules.
05Safety checkReplies are checked before posting. Bad ones never reach chat.
24/7joins within ~2 min of going live
31behaviour tests every week
$0to run — free tiers only
5 minmax timeout — no permanent bans
