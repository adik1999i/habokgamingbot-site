Privacy Policy

HabokGamingBot · Last updated 18 October 2026

HabokGamingBot ("the bot") is a chat moderation and assistant bot that runs only in the live streams of the Habok Gaming YouTube channel. This page explains what information it uses.

What the bot accesses

The bot signs in to its own YouTube account (HabokGamingBot) using Google OAuth with the youtube.force-ssl permission. It uses this access only to read the public live chat of Habok Gaming streams, post chat messages, delete chat messages and apply short chat timeouts. It does not access anyone else's Google account, email, files or private data.

What the bot stores

For moderation, statistics and finding highlight moments for Shorts, the bot keeps a log on its server: viewers' public YouTube channel IDs and display names, message counts, and the text of live chat messages with timestamps. This information is already public in the live chat. Raw chat messages are automatically deleted after 30 days. The data is used only to run the bot and is not sold or shared.

Highlight detection

After a stream, the bot looks for moments where chat reacted strongly and asks Google's Gemini API to review those short parts of the public stream recording, to suggest ideas for Shorts.

AI processing

Messages that mention the bot are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

Removal

To ask for your information to be removed from the bot's log, email adityak1999i@gmail.com with your YouTube channel name.
